# Motivation baseline ladder — spider2 terminus x2, 750K pool, fcfs

Generated by `analysis/ladder_report.py` from `scripts/ladder/run_ladder_tok.sh` (v2, 2026-09-28). **Every method replays the same server-side token input**: the ids one GPU arm of that point actually sent/generated (`scripts/ladder/make_token_stream.py` = `des_sim.ids_from_gpu`), so only the timing / scheduling model differs between methods. Scoring = warm turns, 300 s head / 60 s tail cut, same as `gpu_windows.py`.

- **B1** trace-driven cache replay (`offline_replay.py`): every turn at its recorded time and recorded service time, trials re-packed to the target concurrency (`retime_for_concurrency`); no server model, so no TTFT.
- **B2** open-loop DES (`scripts/ladder/b2_openloop.py --no-stitch`): des_sim's scheduler/cache/step costs, but each (program, turn) arrives at B1's recorded time; completions never feed back.
- **B3** naive closed loop (`closed_loop_sim.py`, defaults incl. decode_overlap 0.5) with its 6 step-cost parameters least-squares projected from the same machine profile (`scripts/ladder/fit_b3_params.py`); no host-load model.
- **DES** des_sim closed loop, **SGL** sgl_sim closed loop (real SGLang Scheduler + event_loop_overlap, via `scripts/ladder/sgl_tok.py`); both mean ± sd over 3 noise seeds (10 at pod c48), v1 machine profiles (`local8xh100.json`, `pod0920.json`), host-load timeline of the token arm (local box only).
- **GPU** `gpu_windows.py` on the arm's server.log (local8xh100 = shared 8×H100 box; pod0920 = cloud pod).
- **Host load is not modelled**: every simulator runs at its machine profile's conditions (no `--load-from`). GPU runs whose host load (cotenant.log `load=`) was outside the profile's envelope -- median > 100 or p10–p90 spread > 60 -- are marked **环境超出档案条件**; they are listed but not used for conclusions, and their gaps are not read as simulator error.
- **token** = GPU arm whose ids the point replays (all methods, all policy arms). `*` = borrowed from another concurrency/seed (sim-only points, or a point whose GPU arm is another seed) — same stream, same client type where possible; GPU arms of one point agree on 74–98 % of outputs and on every prompt length.
- **own** = a row whose GPU arm was run separately from the point's token arm replays that GPU arm's own tokens (`--own-runs`). Two GPU runs of one point can drop different programs mid-run (client ServerDisconnectedError), so they are different workloads: local c46 LRU dropped 93 turns of one program, RR-C+D 90 turns of two others; on the common turns the two token streams give the same B1 miss to the digit. The Δ table below always compares arms on ONE token stream.

## Findings (v2, 2026-09-28: GPU tokens, no host-load model; c46 / pod c48 revised the same evening)

Conclusions use only GPU points run at normal host load: local c32 s0 (all three arms), c32 s1 RR-C+D, c46 LRU and c46 RR-C+D (each on its own GPU tokens), and pod c40 / c48. Points marked **环境超出档案条件** — local c24, c32 s1/s2 LRU and LRU+D, c48 LRU — are listed below, not read as simulator error.

1. **The slack offset is gone; it was a token-input artefact.** v1 had every simulator, B1 included, 0.5–1.3 pp below the GPU at c24/c32. With the GPU's own tokens, des_sim and sgl_sim sit within ±0.07 pp at c32 s0 (3.46 / 3.43 vs 3.43 LRU; 3.24 / 3.19 vs 3.26 RR-C+D). B3 is within +0.12 pp. U p99 is within ±4 %. The local c32 policy delta now has the GPU's sign (Δmiss −0.12 / −0.16 pp vs −0.06; ΔU p99 +17 / +15 % vs +21 %).
2. **Open loop underestimates at the wall and overestimates at slack.**
   - Pod c48 wall: B1 is −11.5 / −8.8 pp, B2 −7.5 / −5.9 pp.
   - Slack, on the same tokens: B1 is +0.36 to +0.50 pp and B2 +0.7 to +2.6 pp. At the recorded times, turns land on a cache state the closed loop never reaches.
   - B2's TTFT diverges once there is any queue: 35–46 s at local c46–48 and 32 s at pod c48, against 0.2–1.3 s on the GPU.
3. **B3 (naive closed loop, equal calibration) still under-amplifies at the wall.**
   - Pod c48: 7.3 / 7.6 vs 15.4 / 13.9 %. Local c46 RR-C+D: −2.6 pp.
   - At slack it is within ±0.12 pp, but its TTFT is 28–89 % too fast everywhere.
   - "Naive closed loop over-amplifies" still does not hold with a fair calibration.
4. **Pod c48 wall (host load 11, normal): both simulators' seed distributions cover the GPU magnitude, neither resolves the policy sign.** 10 noise seeds; each arm on its own GPU tokens.
   - LRU+D: sgl_sim 13.97 ± 1.86 (−1.46 pp, −0.8 sd), des_sim 17.28 ± 2.61 (+1.85 pp, +0.7 sd). RR-C+D: sgl_sim 16.82 ± 2.12 (+2.96, +1.4 sd), des_sim 18.82 ± 1.80 (+4.95, +2.7 sd). v1's snapshot sgl_sim run was −3.56 / +4.44.
   - The two GPU arms dropped different turns (LRU+D 9, RR-C+D 21, incl. 7 turns of a 52K-context program). Replaying the RR-C+D arm's tokens instead of the LRU+D arm's raises the simulated wall by 1.5–2 pp for both arms in both simulators: at this point a dozen dropped turns move the wall as much as the policy does.
   - Policy delta (RR-C+D − LRU+D, paired by noise seed, mean ± se): des_sim −0.92 ± 0.37 (8/10 seeds negative) on the LRU+D arm's tokens and −0.12 ± 0.91 (5/10) on the RR-C+D arm's; sgl_sim +1.57 ± 0.87 (2/10) and +0.81 ± 0.69 (4/10). GPU −1.56 is one run per arm. The earlier "only des_sim gets the pod c48 sign" came from three seeds and is withdrawn.
   - Window by window (`analysis/wall_windows.py`): onset (10–15 min) and the LRU+D recovery match — GPU 51 → 28 → 3.4 % in 15–30 min, sgl_sim 38 → 28 → 3.3 %. The one clear mismatch is the GPU RR-C+D run's recovery: 61 → 15 % in 15–25 min, while 35 of 40 simulated RR-C+D runs are still above 20 % in 20–25 min (lowest 18 %). Per minute (`analysis/wave_minutes.py`) the stretch is two waves at the same context demand (~1000K, 1.33× the pool): 14–19 min, where every arm and method sits at 43–68 %, and 21–26 min, which is **bistable per run**. A simulated run either escapes it (12–25 %) or falls in (40–70 %), under both policies. Wave-2 miss GPU LRU+D 30.0 / RR-C+D 14.5 %; runs at or below 25 % — sgl_sim LRU+D 7/10 and 3/10 on the two token streams, RR-C+D 3/10 and 2/10; des_sim 0–2/10 each. The GPU RR-C+D run is the escape branch, which the simulators produce but rarely. At this point a policy acts by shifting the escape probability, so one GPU run per arm is one draw of it, and the GPU Δ −1.56 pp cannot be read as the policy effect.
   - TTFT p90: LRU+D sgl_sim −39 % / des_sim +8 %; RR-C+D +57 % / +96 % (the slow recovery above).
5. **Local c46 (loads 48 / 34, normal): the old −2 pp RR-C+D gap was a workload difference, not simulator error.**
   - The two GPU arms of this point were run on different days and the client dropped different programs mid-run (ServerDisconnectedError): the LRU run lost 93 turns of salesforce001 (context up to 41.6K), the RR-C+D run lost 85 turns of shopify002 (44K) and 5 of hive001. Everything else agrees: prompt lengths are identical turn by turn, the infinite-pool floor is 1.90 vs 1.87 %, and restricted to the 5267 common turns the two token streams give the same B1 miss to the digit (LRU 4.86, RR-C+D 4.43).
   - Each arm on its OWN GPU tokens (row marked "own"): LRU des_sim +0.63 / sgl_sim +0.42 pp; RR-C+D des_sim +0.85 / sgl_sim +0.15 pp (U p99 +17 / +1 %). On the LRU arm's tokens RR-C+D had been −1.73 / −2.17 pp.
   - Consequently the GPU's c46 "policy delta" (RR-C+D 6.85 vs LRU 5.04 %) is not a policy comparison. On one token stream the simulators put RR-C+D 0.55–0.94 pp below LRU and 0.23–0.69 pp above LRU+D, on either stream (Δ table, two c46 rows).
   - Both simulators are +21–33 % slow on TTFT p90 at c46.
6. **Host-load-flagged points, listed only.**
   - c48 LRU (load 27–111): des_sim −0.41, sgl_sim −1.46 pp.
   - c24 / c32 s1 / c32 s2 LRU and LRU+D: des_sim and sgl_sim within ±0.07 pp of the GPU.

**Shape verdict (v2):**
- **Holds:** the open-loop classes (B1, B2) miss the capacity wall by 6–11 pp and misplace slack points; naive closed loop under-amplifies; the scheduler-faithful closed loop reproduces slack within ±0.1 pp, and at the pod wall its seed distribution covers the GPU run (−0.8 to +1.4 sd for sgl_sim).
- **Does not hold yet:** "ours within noise at every point": des_sim's RR-C+D at the pod wall is +2.7 sd, and no simulator resolves the pod c48 policy sign (10 seeds, two token streams); the GPU side has one run per arm, so a wall-point policy claim needs GPU repeats. (Local c46 RR-C+D, −1.7 to −2.2 pp in the first v2 pass, is +0.15 / +0.85 pp once it replays its own tokens.)
- **Method rule:** compare a GPU arm only with simulations of its own tokens, and compare two GPU arms only if they dropped the same programs (or on their common turns).

Resource peak for this pass: 3 simulation processes × 2 threads, ≈ 12 GB (token parse 3.6 GB per process). Runs: `scratchpad/ladder_tok/runs_noload/`; c46 own-token rows `runs_owntok/` (`POINTS='add_point local8xh100 46 0 $ALL sp46rr' RUNS=runs_owntok`), common-turn check `runs_x/`; pod c48 seeds 4–10 `NSEEDS="4 5 6 7 8 9 10"`, pod c48 own-token rows `runs_owntok/pod0920`. The earlier pass with a host-load timeline (97 csv, stopped part-way) is kept in `scratchpad/ladder_tok/runs_withload/` for comparison only.

## Previous pass (v1, kept for comparison)

v1 differed in the token input and in sgl_sim: prediction mode on the RECORDED stream (des_sim's stitching emulation with program-unique outputs; the local c24/c32/c48 GPU arms were run by the pre-2026-09-20 unstitched client, so that emulation does not describe them), and sgl_sim was a snapshot run with the normal (non-overlap) loop, no noise model, no host load. Its findings:

Scope: one workload (spider2 terminus x2) and one pool (750K); GPU points: local LRU c24/c32×3 seeds/c46/c48 and cloud LRU+D/RR-C+D c40/c48. The c32 bars show seed-to-seed spread (seeds 0–2), not same-config repeats.

1. **B1 (recorded-time cache replay) loses the capacity wall.** It stays within about 0.5 pp of the GPU while the system is slack. At the wall it underestimates 2.7–3.9×: local c48 LRU 5.6 vs 15.1 %, pod c48 LRU+D 4.0 vs 15.4 %. From c46 to c48 its miss barely moves (4.9 → 5.6), while the GPU jumps from 5.0 to 15.1 %. It is close at local c46 (4.85 vs 5.04) only because it sits below the knee.
2. **B2 (open-loop DES) is wrong in both directions.**
   - Slack: it overestimates by +0.6 pp at c24/c32, and by +1.9 pp at pod c40. The recorded arrival times come from a 4-concurrent recording with shorter service times. At higher concurrency the turns get slower, so 58–91 % of turns arrive before their own previous turn has finished; those turns carry 63–74 % of the warm uncached tokens.
   - Wall: it underestimates by 6.6–7.5 pp.
   - TTFT: arrivals do not back off when the server falls behind, so the queue grows without bound. TTFT p90 reaches 46–53 s at c46–48, against 0.2–1.3 s on the GPU.
3. **B3 (naive closed loop, calibrated step costs) does NOT over-amplify on this sweep; it under-amplifies.** Local c48 is 11.7 vs 15.1 %; pod c48 is 7.3 vs 15.4 %. TTFT is 30–90 % too fast because of its decode_overlap 0.5 and its coarser prefill form. The earlier "4× over-amplification" (mldev c42) came from its original hand-tuned parameters. Once B3 gets the same machine calibration, that claim does not reproduce here, so the motivation must not state it as general.
4. **The closed-loop, scheduler-faithful simulators (DES, SGL) follow the wall, but they are not "within noise" at every point.**
   - Right onset and magnitude: local c48 LRU 12.7 / 13.0 vs 15.1 %; pod c40 ±0.15 pp; pod c48 LRU+D DES 19.1 vs 15.4 %.
   - Local c46 is too deep: 8.9 / 8.4 vs 5.0 % for LRU.
   - DES TTFT p90 is within ±15 % at slack points and 30–65 % off at the wall.
   - Only DES gets the one meaningful policy decision right: at pod c48, RR-C+D minus LRU+D is −1.51 pp / −5 % vs GPU −1.56 pp / −9 %. SGL gives +6.4 pp there; it is a single noise-free run, and wall points in this regime are known to be chaotic.
5. **All simulators, B1 included, sit 0.5–1.3 pp below the GPU at slack points (c24/c32 local).** The gap is independent of timing model, which points to the GPU side. Two known effects fit: prediction mode's stitching emulation assumes the regenerated tokens match the recorded ones up to the stitch point, and the GPU's declared demote is partly blocked by split prefixes. That would also explain why the sims see no LRU+D ≈ RR-C+D difference at c32 (0.00 pp / 0 %) while the GPU shows ΔU p99 +20 %. It should be checked with des_sim `--ids-from` (diagnostic mode) before the slack points are used in the paper.

**Shape verdict:** "open loop underestimates at the wall" holds for B1 and for B2 at the wall. "Naive closed loop over-amplifies" does not hold with a fair calibration. "Ours within noise" holds at slack and at the pod c40/c48 points for DES, but not at local c46. The defensible claim on this data: closed-loop, scheduler-faithful simulation is the only class that reproduces the capacity wall and the wall-point policy decision; the open-loop classes cannot, by construction.
