## Why these points

The pod c48 750K point used in the ladder sits in a ~50K-wide bistable window: the second wall wave escapes or collapses per run (escape 0–1/10 at ≤725K, 3–5/10 at 750K, 10/10 at ≥775K; `analysis/escape_curve.py`). One GPU run per arm there is one draw of an escape probability. The points below are outside that window, where the seed spread is 0.1–0.4 pp and both simulators agree.

Local c46 has no bistable window in the sweep (625–775K): the wave miss falls smoothly, 88 → 65 → 35 → 15 → 7 %, between 650K and 750K.

## What the GPU runs test (local c46, 750K and 775K, LRU+D and RR-C+D, ≥2 runs per arm)

1. Δmiss > 0 at both pools, about +0.3 to +0.5 pp (GPU same-config miss sd on this box ≈ 0.16 pp).
2. ΔU p99 flips sign: about −12 to −15 % at 750K, about +50 to +60 % at 775K.
3. Δn>16k < 0 at both pools: RR-C+D has fewer >16K recomputations, even where it has a higher miss.
4. Absolute LRU+D miss: about 4.5 % at 750K and 3.0 % at 775K.
5. The TTFT deltas (+1 to +2 %) are below the GPU's run-to-run TTFT noise, so they are not tested.

Comparison rules:
- Each GPU arm is compared on its own tokens as a check.
